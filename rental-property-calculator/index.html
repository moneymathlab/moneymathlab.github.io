Quick answers

What is a good cap rate for a rental property?

There is no single number: it depends on the market and the risk. Compare the cap rate with similar properties nearby and with your mortgage rate.

What is cash-on-cash return?

Yearly cash flow divided by the cash you invested (down payment plus closing and repair costs). It shows what your own money earns.

What is DSCR?

Debt service coverage ratio: net operating income divided by the mortgage payment. Above 1.0 the rent covers the loan; many lenders look for about 1.2 or more.
