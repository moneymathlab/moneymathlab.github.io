Quick answers

How much do you save with the 100 envelope challenge?

$5,050 in the classic version (1 + 2 + … + 100). The half version saves $2,525, the double version $10,100.

How long does the 100 envelope challenge take?

It depends on your pace: 2 envelopes a week takes 50 weeks, 7 a week takes about 14 weeks (100 days).

How much is that per paycheck?

For the classic $5,050 at 2 envelopes a week, about $101 a week, or about $202 per paycheck if you are paid every 2 weeks.
