Quick answers

How much will no tax on overtime save me?

It depends on your tax bracket. The deduction lowers taxable income, so a single filer in the 12% bracket with a $2,400 overtime premium saves about $288. The same premium saves about $528 in the 22% bracket.

Is all my overtime pay tax-free?

No. Only the premium counts: the extra half above your regular rate on overtime required by the Fair Labor Standards Act. If you earn $20 an hour and $30 for overtime, $10 per overtime hour is deductible. The deduction is capped at $12,500 ($25,000 on a joint return). Social Security and Medicare tax still apply.

Where do I find my qualified overtime for 2026?

Starting with tax year 2026, employers report qualified overtime on Form W-2, Box 12, code TT (Form 1099-MISC box 14 or 1099-NEC box 1d for non-employees).

What are the income limits?

The tips and overtime deductions shrink by $100 for each $1,000 of modified AGI over $150,000 ($300,000 joint). The car loan interest deduction shrinks by $200 per $1,000 over $100,000 ($200,000 joint). The $6,000 senior deduction is reduced by 6% of modified AGI over $75,000 ($150,000 joint).

Who cannot claim it?

Married people filing separately cannot claim the tips, overtime or senior deductions, and a work-valid Social Security number is required. The deductions apply to tax years 2025 through 2028.

Do I need to itemize?

No. The four Schedule 1-A deductions work with the standard deduction or with itemized deductions.
