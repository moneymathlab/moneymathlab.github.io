Quick answers

What is the difference between the debt snowball and the debt avalanche?

Both pay the minimum on every debt and put the extra money on one debt at a time. Snowball targets the smallest balance first for a faster first win; avalanche targets the highest interest rate first, which usually costs less interest.

Which one saves more money?

The avalanche usually costs less interest; the snowball usually gives you a paid-off debt sooner. The gap depends on your balances and rates, so compare both with your own numbers.

What happens to a minimum payment when a debt is paid off?

In both methods it rolls into the next target debt, so your total monthly payment stays the same and the payoff speeds up.
