Quick answers

How much tax will I save on $8,000 of tips?

It depends on your bracket. In the 12% bracket a single filer saves about $960; in the 22% bracket, about $1,760. The calculator above uses the 2026 brackets.

Who qualifies for the tips deduction?

Workers in jobs that customarily received tips on the Treasury list (servers, bartenders, hairstylists, delivery drivers and others), with a work-valid Social Security number. Married people must file jointly.

What is the limit?

Up to $25,000 of qualified tips per return. The deduction shrinks by $100 for every $1,000 of modified AGI above $150,000 ($300,000 joint).

Do automatic service charges count?

No. Only voluntary tips that the customer chooses to give count as qualified tips.

Do I still pay Social Security and Medicare on tips?

Yes. The deduction only lowers federal income tax.
