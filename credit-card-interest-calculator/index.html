Quick answers

How is credit card interest calculated?

Most issuers apply a daily rate (APR divided by 365) to your average daily balance. A simple monthly estimate is balance × APR ÷ 12: $6,500 at 24.99% APR is about $135 of interest in the first month.

How long does it take to pay off $6,500 at 24.99% APR with $300 a month?

About 30 months, with roughly $2,230 of interest, using a monthly estimate. Paying more each month cuts both the time and the interest.

Do I pay interest if I pay the statement balance in full?

Usually not on purchases: paying the full statement balance by the due date keeps the grace period. Cash advances and some balance transfers can still charge interest. Check your card agreement.
