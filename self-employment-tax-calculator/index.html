Quick answers

How is self-employment tax calculated in 2027?

Multiply net profit by 92.35%, then apply 12.4% Social Security up to the 2027 wage base of $192,300 and 2.9% Medicare on all of it. Half of the total is deductible when you figure your income tax.

How much should a freelancer set aside for taxes?

Self-employment tax alone is close to 14.1% of profit; income tax and state tax come on top. The full set-aside depends on your profit, filing status and state.

Do I need to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year and no tax is withheld, the IRS generally expects four estimated payments.
