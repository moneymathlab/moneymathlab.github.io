Quick answers

How much should I save each month for my wedding?

Take what is still to save (budget minus what you already have) and divide it by the full months left before the wedding. With a $30,000 budget, $8,000 saved and 10 months to go, that is $2,200 a month.

How do I work out my wedding cost per guest?

Divide the total budget by the number of guests you plan to invite. A $30,000 budget for 110 guests is about $272.73 per guest. Cutting the list is usually the fastest way to lower the total.

Should a wedding budget include a contingency?

A buffer for surprises (extra guests, overtime, last-minute fees) keeps you from going over. The calculator lets you choose your own percentage and shows what is left for vendors.
